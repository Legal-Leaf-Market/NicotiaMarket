Tobacco-leaf-free. Not nicotine-free. Both, clearly labelled.

Long cut and pouches, made in North Carolina from barn-cured leafy greens instead of tobacco leaf. Their core line still contains nicotine; their separate ZERO line contains none. Those are two very different products and most write-ups blur them, so this page keeps them apart, using Black Buffalo's own product labelling, not our reading of the name.

Core line

Contains nicotine

Barn-cured leafy greens, food-grade ingredients and pharmaceutical-grade nicotine. No tobacco leaf or stem, but the nicotine is fully there, and Black Buffalo say each pouch carries the same nicotine content as a traditional pouch.

ZERO line

No nicotine at all

A separate range with no nicotine and no tobacco. Same formats and flavours, none of the active ingredient. If you came here looking for the nicotine-free one, this is it, and it is the only one.

about the per-pouch number

Why we show it as an estimate

Everything on this site is priced by the unit, so a can of pouches wants a per-pouch figure. The problem: Black Buffalo do not publish an exact count. Their own FAQ says approximately 16 per can, and it says approximately because the cans are filled by weight, not counted. An independent count found between 14 and 18.

So we show price per can as the hard number, because that one is just division and it is exact. Every per-pouch figure is marked as an estimate at 16. At the real-world range of 14 to 18, a five-can roll works out somewhere between roughly $0.31 and $0.39 a pouch. We would rather show you that spread than print a confident number that is quietly wrong.

Long cut is not affected and gets no per-pouch price at all. It is loose dip sold by weight, so a "per pouch" figure for it would be an invented number.
