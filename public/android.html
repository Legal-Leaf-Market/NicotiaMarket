Put the market on your phone

Every store, priced by the unit, one tap from your home screen. No account needed to browse.

1 · Add to your home screen

The fastest way, and it updates itself — no reinstalling when we ship a fix.

On your phone, open your browser's menu and choose Add to Home Screen.

2 · Or download the APK

A real installed app, straight from us. We are not on the Play Store — Google restricts apps that facilitate nicotine sales, so this is a direct download.

Download the APK

Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time — that is normal for any app not from the Play Store. Tap Settings → Allow from this source, then open the downloaded file.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. 21+ only.

On an iPhone?

There is no App Store listing — Apple's rules bar apps that facilitate tobacco or vape sales. Open nicotiamarket.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. You get the same app.
