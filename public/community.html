Four rules, and only four

  1. 21+. You confirm it before your first post. We do not check ID and we are not pretending to, but if you are under 21, this is not for you.
  2. No sales, trades or meetups. Not one. Do not offer product, do not ask for product, do not arrange to meet a stranger. Posts that look like it get held automatically. This is the rule we will never bend.
  3. No contact details. Yours or anybody else's, no email, no number, no handles for other apps. Held automatically, for your safety more than ours.
  4. Do not be a bastard. The whole premise of this place is that it is where the people who got sent outside end up. Act like it.
