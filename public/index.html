Shopping - stores who they are, where they ship

We hold no stock. Every item ships from the store selling it, at that store's price, under its own returns policy and age checks. Prices are shown per pouch, per 1,000 puffs, per ml and per stick so packs, bundles and big-puff disposables compare honestly.

How we make money

We earn a commission when you buy through our links. It never changes your price. Ranking is by unit price, not by what pays us, that's the whole point of showing cost per pouch, per puff and per stick.

Who can buy

Adults 21 and over who already use tobacco or nicotine. If you don't, don't start.

Rules differ by country, state and city. Snus can't be sold in most of the EU outside Sweden. Some stores can't ship to your address whatever this page shows. The store verifies your age at checkout.
