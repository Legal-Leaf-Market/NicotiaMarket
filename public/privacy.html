Privacy

what we collect,and where it stays

This is written in plain English on purpose. It describes what the site actually does, which we can be precise about because we wrote it. Last updated 8 August 2026.

I

the short version

No ad trackers, no profile, nothing sold

There is no advertising network on this site, no tag manager, no session recorder, no chat widget and no data broker. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information to anybody, and we never have.

Almost everything the site remembers about you (your cart, your saved items, where you ship to) lives in your own browser and never reaches us. The exceptions are things you type into a box and send on purpose: an email address, or a post on the community board. Both are described below, precisely.

II

what stays on your device

The keys, enumerated

The site keeps a short list of values in your browser so it can remember what you were doing between visits. Because we wrote the code, we can list them all, by name:

Most of these are stored twice: in your browser's local storage, and as a first-party cookie that expires after 30 days, because some browser setups block one and allow the other. Full honesty about how cookies work: a cookie travels with your requests to our own domain, and our server does not read or keep any of them. None is readable by any other site, and none tracks you anywhere.

The app also caches its own files (the page, the styles, the engine) so it opens instantly and survives a dropped connection. That cache is copies of our code, not data about you.

You can wipe all of it at any time by clearing site data for this domain in your browser settings. Nothing breaks; the site forgets you and the age gate asks again.

III

what you send us on purpose

The email box, and the board

The email signup

If you give us an email address, we record the address, any name you typed, the country and region you had set, and when. It goes to our mailing list, which lives in a Google spreadsheet we control, so we can email you. That is all it is for. There is no password, ever; the sign-in on this site is your email and nothing else, and it is convenience, not authentication.

The community board

Posting on The Back Door sends us the name you typed, your post, and your confirmation that you are 21 or over. All of it is public by design: no accounts, no email, no DMs. Do not post contact details; the filter holds them anyway, for your safety as much as anyone else's.

To be removed from the mailing list, email hello@nicotiamarket.com and say so. We will delete the record; we do not require a reason and we will not try to talk you out of it.

IV

the technical record

What our server sees

Anonymous events. When you add to cart or hand off to a store's checkout, the page sends our server a small event: what happened, which store, which product, the value, the country you had set, and when. No name, no email, no account id, no cookie. We use it to see which shelves work, not who is shopping.

Page counts. We use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without building a profile of you across sites. We use it to see which pages people read, not who read them.

The board and IP addresses. The community board uses your IP address for rate limiting: a per-minute counter that lives about two minutes and a per-hour counter that lives about two hours, so nobody can flood the board. Published posts do not store your IP. If you report a post, the report keeps the reporter's IP with it (only the most recent reports are kept), so abuse of the report button is traceable.

The site is hosted by Vercel, and like any web host their servers keep ordinary request logs, which include IP addresses. We do not use those logs to identify anyone, but they exist and we would be misleading you to leave them out.

The typefaces come from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google to fetch them, and Google sees that request. If that matters to you, it is worth knowing.

VI

when you leave

Clicking through to a store

When you click a product link you go to that store's own website, and from that point you are on their site under their privacy policy and their age check, not ours. Some of those links carry an affiliate tag, which tells the store the visit came from us so that any commission is credited. It does not tell them who you are, because we do not know either.

If a commission is later credited, the affiliate network tells us that an order happened and what it was worth. It does not tell us who placed it. What you buy, and everything you type into a store's checkout, stays between you and that store.

VII

asking us things

Your data, and how to get rid of it

Email hello@nicotiamarket.com and ask. You can ask what we hold on you, ask for a copy, or ask us to delete it. In practice, for almost everybody, the honest answer will be "an email address, and it is gone now."

This site is for adults 21 and over. We do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 21, and if we learn that we have, we will delete it.

If we change how any of this works, we will change this page and move the date at the top. We are not going to quietly widen what we collect and leave the old page up.
